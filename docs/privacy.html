Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

MarkMind is made by Phillip Hatzenbichler. In short: MarkMind does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your notes stay on your device.

Your notes and files

MarkMind works on the folders you open. Notes, attachments, the change history and settings are stored on your device, in those folders or in the app's settings folder. They are never sent to us. If you keep a folder in iCloud Drive or another sync service, that service handles the syncing under its own terms.

No accounts, analytics or ads

MarkMind has no user accounts. It does not include analytics, tracking or advertising, and it does not use third party SDKs that collect data.

Tips

Tips are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or other personal information. Apple's privacy policy applies to these purchases.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use MarkMind with Siri or Shortcuts, your requests are processed by Apple as part of those features. MarkMind only reads or writes the notes you ask for, on your device.

Tools you run yourself

On the Mac, MarkMind includes a terminal and can start command line tools you install yourself, such as AI coding agents or local model runners. These tools are not part of MarkMind. What they do with your data is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. MarkMind's connection to them runs locally on your Mac.

Children

MarkMind does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/haetzen/MarkMind/issues.
