Support

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? Happy to hear it.

Get in touch

Open an issue on GitHub: github.com/haetzen/MarkMind/issues. Please mention your device, your system version and the Notewright version (Notewright menu, About Notewright).

Common questions

Where are my notes stored?
In the folder you opened in Notewright, as normal Markdown files. Notewright keeps its own data, like the change history, in a hidden .markmind folder inside it.

How do I get an older version of a note back?
On the Mac, open the history panel (clock icon at the top right), pick a version and click Restore This Version. The current text is saved to the history first, so you can undo this.

How do I connect my coding agent?
Open Settings, Agents, and click Connect next to your folder. Then restart the agent in the terminal. It can then open notes, change settings and set up jobs in Notewright.

Why is there no terminal on iPhone and iPad?
iOS does not allow apps to run command line programs, so the terminal, the change history and scheduled jobs are Mac only. Notes edited on the Mac show up on your other devices if the folder is synced, for example with iCloud Drive.

A job I did not create shows "Needs review".
Jobs created or changed by an agent stay switched off until you approve them in Scheduled Jobs. Check the command, then click Approve and Enable, or delete the job.

Do tips unlock anything?
No. Tips are a way to support development.
